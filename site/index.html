Conditional Freedom

How American Ideals and Strategic Interests Shaped U.S.–Middle East Relations, 1776–2001

The United States began by defending its own independence and commercial interests, later promoted political self-government as an international ideal, and eventually acquired enough power to influence whether Middle Eastern governments survived, changed, or received American military protection.
